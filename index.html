How it works

It lives where your team already works

No new habit to enforce. basezero connects to the tools people already use, and is reachable from inside them — with a wiki-like view of accumulated knowledge sitting behind it.

01

Connect

Email, Slack, Claude, and call-notetaker tools like Jamie. Knowledge is picked up from the work itself, not from a folder someone remembered to fill.

02

Reach it anywhere

Ask through Claude, ask a Slackbot, or open the basezero interface directly. Behind all three sits a wiki-like view of everything the graph has accumulated.

03

Inbox

Inconsistencies and contradictions between sources are surfaced as items, each with a drafted fix attached. A person reviews the draft and approves it or rejects it.

04

Share center

Suggestions for what individual knowledge should move to which team, and why. Nothing moves from personal to organizational until a person approves that specific move.

A person approves every change. This is a human-in-the-loop workflow, not an automated sync. basezero drafts, proposes and explains; the decision to publish a fix or move a fact from personal to organizational is always made by someone, on the record.

Technical capabilities

Knowledge in one system, owned by you

Most AI tools search a folder. This is a knowledge graph — it ingests your organization’s knowledge, resolves it into a single structure, reasons across it, and keeps proving it’s right. Five layers, working together.

01
INGESTION

One graph, kept current, automatically

Every source — AFAS, Exact, DATEV, Personio, SharePoint, Drive, Slack, your file shares — syncs on a schedule, not a one-time import. As content lands, it’s resolved into entities and typed relationships, so “your biggest customer” is one node no matter how many systems mention them. The graph tracks the entity, not the file. No manual uploads. No stale context six weeks after go-live. No migration project every time a source changes.

Incremental syncChange detectionDeletion propagationPermission refreshEntity resolution
02
RETRIEVAL

Finds the whole answer, and says so

Most assistants fire a single query and have no way to know what they missed — there’s nothing in their architecture to enumerate against. This layer knows the edges of its own corpus. It walks all of it, follows a question across as many hops as the answer requires, and reports what it covered, not just what it found. Ask the same question about last year and it retrieves what was true then, not only what’s true now. Every claim traces back to the passage it came from.

Exhaustive scanMulti-hop reasoningTemporalityProvenanceCoverage reporting
03
TRANSPARENCY

See the answer. See the evidence. See the source.

Every answer arrives with its ranked sources and a direct line to the underlying documents — not a black box you have to take on faith. What the graph concludes on top of your raw data is written to a living wiki: one page per customer, product or process, every claim linked back to where it came from. Anyone can open it, search it, correct it in place. No embeddings you can’t inspect, no query language to learn, nothing hidden.

Ranked source setDirect document accessPassage-level citationsWiki pagesEditable in place
04
CURATION

Gets better because people use it

The graph watches itself — surfacing gaps, contradictions and pages going stale, with the missing piece already drafted for a person to approve. It also learns from how your team actually communicates, reasoning across the graph instead of only retrieving what’s already written down. Most AI tools write what they learn into a private memory only one person can read. This one gets smarter for whoever asks next.

Gap & contradiction detectionDrafted fixesHuman approvalWrite-back to your filesOrg-level learningGraph inference
05
CONTROL

Every permission, all the way down

Permissions live on the fact, not just the file — and they propagate into everything built from it. A summary inherits the combined rights of every source behind it; revoke one document and every answer derived from it loses access too. Sensitive content is tagged and rerouted before it ever reaches a model that shouldn’t see it. Hosted in the EU or in your own cloud, fully exportable, always. Your knowledge stays yours. No lock-in.
